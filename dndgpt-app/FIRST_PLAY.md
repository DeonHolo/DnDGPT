# First play with your configured connection

Complete the hosting, OAuth and actual-client checks in the project README first. This repository does not include a working account binding.

1. Open regular ChatGPT and select your installed DnDGPT plugin/App.
2. Sign in to your own save account when prompted.
3. Ask: "Start a fresh DnDGPT game under the complete edition 1.0 rules. Use the dashboard and saved roll tools. Begin character creation only; wait for my separate decisions to confirm the character and start the adventure."
4. Make the creation choices in Chat. Ask to open the dashboard; its pages show the saved results.
5. Review and confirm the finished character, then separately authorize the adventure.
6. Play in Chat. The GM batches resolved changes into a save; inspecting dashboard pages never uses a turn.

One conversation is enough. Import/export is optional; saved dashboard data is what lets the view reopen reliably. Narrated changes appear there after the save succeeds. Restore a failed connection before relying on displayed state.

Your hosting process must remain available. How it starts after a reboot depends on your installation. The author's personal Windows startup/tunnel configuration is intentionally excluded from public source.
