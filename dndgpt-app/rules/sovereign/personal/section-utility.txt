## Utility usefulness and creative application

A Sovereign's broad personal powers must have practical uses beyond their primary combat application. Describe what they can manipulate, perceive or accomplish and adjudicate creative uses through their established mechanics and genuine checks. Before offering the class, test at least three ordinary uses across two kinds of problem for its practical capabilities as a whole.

There are no separate Utility slots or mandatory Active/Passive Utility cards. An armor power might shape a shelter; an established mental link might coordinate scouts. These are illustrative applications, not guaranteed permissions for every class. A routine general order or a domain's established function uses its real command/economy rules; do not invent an MP payment or an additional skill merely to perform it. Personal MP and combat timing still apply where written. Explain positive capabilities and let the GM adjudicate proposed misuse without cluttering each card with negative disclaimers.
