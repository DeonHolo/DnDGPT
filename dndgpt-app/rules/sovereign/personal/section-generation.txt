## Generation breadth, reaction roles and a playable world

Introduce the actual region before Luck: its physical environment, travel, ordinary life, local magic/technology, public pressures and concrete connection to Earth. Save these public facts in World. Offer multiple possible pursuits; keep hidden causes private and leave the player's biography to them.

Generate distinct operating rules before names. Each sovereign connects personal adventure to its actual domain, commanders and forces in a different way: acquisition, production, recovery, expansion and command must create different decisions. Compare the eight concepts and genuinely accessible past offers by trigger, operation, payoff and practical use. Repeated marking, echoes or changed damage types do not establish novelty. Redesign concepts before rolling; repair an inadequate published design at its already fixed rarity. Never invent comparisons with unavailable chats.

Build four broad personal powers around that law. Their combined kit must support useful independent offense, survivability, movement and practical exploration appropriate to the class; these are whole-kit outcomes, not four prescribed slots or a mandatory role per power. Powers can mix deliberate uses, automatic behavior and responses within their own identity. Keep actual Action, Reaction, movement and resource budgets explicit in source rules. A response may disrupt or attack; do not default every class to the same defensive trigger. Basic attacks and ordinary orders remain available under their normal rules without becoming more named skills.

Test useful applications in several ordinary situations. Do not create a narrow power that only works at one scripted obstacle or invent future scenes to justify it. Practical out-of-combat functions belong to the established powers and the actual domain/forces. No separate Utility acquisition/category quota applies.
