# 10. SOVEREIGN TITLES

Offer five meaningful Titles on the third creation turn; choose one or NONE. Preserve the eligible creation rarity override and independent natural receipts. A Title expresses earned identity, reputation or a class-fitting aspiration. Its mechanical contribution is ONE clearly documented refinement attached to an actual personal power, with the Title's own rarity and provenance; it creates no extra standalone power or action budget. Show the identity in Character & Progression and its effect in the parent's Upgrades and consolidated rules. Existing Titles retain their actual recorded effects. Selecting NONE is fully legal.
