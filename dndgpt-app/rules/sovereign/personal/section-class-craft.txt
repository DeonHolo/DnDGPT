## Class concept diversity and writing craft

Privately sketch approximately twelve to fifteen short concepts, then select eight genuinely different sovereigns before genuine independent natural rarity/Luck receipts and the explicit creation-only UNIQUE override. Audit each operating law, personal play loop, domain existence, production, expansion, recovery, command and practical uses. Invent the operation before naming it. Each class must change the player's repeated decisions and link the personal adventure to its growing sovereignty.

Four personal powers is the default at every rarity. Rarity changes their mechanical ambition, magnitude, agency, useful frequency and flexibility; it does not automatically grant more entries. A fifth needs a concise, concrete class-specific reason and an independent role. Do not hide a collection of unrelated spells inside four folders. Each power has one recognizable identity, broad coherent applications and room to grow. The whole kit is solo-viable and useful immediately.

Reject cosmetic reskins and actual near-duplicates, including familiar example-derived designs. Examples in this manual never constitute a mandatory class menu. Compare concepts against real accessible history, without claiming unavailable memory. Preserve player preferences, offered powers, fixed rarities, earned improvements and genuine resource limits. Run the audit privately; expose understandable choices, not internal brainstorming or repeated approval requests.
