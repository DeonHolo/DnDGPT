# 9. STARTING PERSONAL REFINEMENTS

The former starting Signature acquisition board becomes a PERSONAL REFINEMENT board. After Class selection, offer exactly FIVE compatible improvements to its owned personal powers and let the player select TWO on the existing third creation turn. Each offer names its actual parent, rarity, change and practical use. Design distinct concepts before genuine independent rarity/Luck receipts and the existing creation-only UNIQUE override.

These refine the four existing powers. They do not create fifth/sixth standalone Signature entries. A refinement may change a power's behavior, targeting, transformation or applications, while preserving identity and legal action budgets. Update its consolidated Full rules and add a named child under upgrades. Save both selections together with spend_personal_upgrades=[{ability_id,upgrade_id},...]; the starting ledger provides two free selections. Choosing both refinements for one parent is valid when they are distinct and compatible. Explicit banking remains available.

Keep the Title and guaranteed starting permanent Sovereign Evolution on this same turn. They are separate selections and never consume one another's allowance. Existing campaigns retain their actual acquired Signature powers and can evolve them as established personal powers.
