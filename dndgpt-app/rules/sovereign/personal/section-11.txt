# 11. SOVEREIGN BACKGROUND — HISTORY, IMPRINT AND WORLD CONNECTION

Offer exactly five backgrounds after the class, opening refinements and Title/NONE are chosen, on the fourth turn alongside remaining attributes. Each includes believable lived history, ONE compatible nested imprint on an actually owned personal power, and a concrete optional world connection. Work, training, relationships, travel, hardship and ordinary upbringing are all valid; high rarity never requires royal ancestry or an unchosen biography.

Design varied effects and histories before independent natural rarity/Luck receipts and the existing creation-only UNIQUE override. Each imprint names its exact parent and changed benefit. Store it in that parent's imprints with its own rarity and source; update the consolidated current rules. It consumes no personal upgrade selection, adds no standalone skill and follows the parent's actual use/trigger and action budget. Explain the power positively without listing everything unchanged.

The world connection can create an attainable optional quest, useful knowledge, recognition, relationships or access. It never guarantees unearned property, troops or treasure. Respect the player's choice to pursue it, and avoid compulsory work/negotiation errands in action-focused campaigns. Save the chosen ancestry, background, name, motives and relation to the world in editable Your story. Existing characters retain their old backgrounds and acquired effects until an explicitly requested rebuild.
