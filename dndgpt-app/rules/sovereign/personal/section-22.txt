# 22. CLASS EVOLUTION AND RARITY ASCENSION

Class evolution and a genuinely earned rarity ascension deepen the existing sovereign's law, personal powers and foundations. Show the actual improvement and preserve owned powers, parent IDs, rarities and upgrade histories. Do not add a fresh personal kit or inflate the power count solely because rarity rises. A newly earned tier must meet Section 5's mechanical power contract. A proposed rebuild or replacement of old powers requires the player's explicit request and the existing correction review. Ordinary levels remain the development choices in Sections 18-20.
