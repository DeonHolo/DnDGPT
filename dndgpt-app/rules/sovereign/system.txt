## Sovereign warfare and consequences

Precommit formations and fronts before resolving their phase. A formation records id/name/side (player or enemy), strength, maximum_strength, attack, defense, power, armor, morale, commander_id, front_id, abilities and status. Strength counts functional members/platforms; morale represents cohesion or class-appropriate control. Class-specific limits and production remain binding. A front records id/name, objective, target_progress, player_progress, enemy_progress, terrain_defense and status. Keep only player-known information in visible reports. Apply shared enemy novelty, intelligent targeting, terrain, limited information and agency. Avoid artificial scaling, retroactive reinforcements or result-driven statistic changes.

Use save_chat_update with sovereign_battle={front_id,phase_id,orders,phases}; phases defaults to 1, maximum 3 preauthorized phases. Each order names formation_id, stance (attack/guard/withdraw), and target_id for an attack. Orders cover participating active forces on both sides; enemy orders reflect their actual knowledge and goals. They must be on the named front. Profiles are fixed during an active engagement; genuine prior personal actions can alter the committed situation before the battle begins. Player attacks can disrupt a commander, open a route or destroy a siege asset through the ordinary personal rules; record those causal changes before resolving the affected phase.

Each phase uses the starting active formations for simultaneous attacks. Hit: genuine d20 + attack + commander contribution (min(3,(commander level-1)//3)) against target defense + front terrain_defense +2 if guarding; natural 1 misses and 20 hits. Successful pressure rolls d6; casualties=ceil(attacker strength * power * d6 / (target armor * 20)), capped at the target's remaining strength. Apply all attacks simultaneously. Guarding/withdrawing formations make no attack; withdrawal routes the formation after incoming attacks. After losses, reduce morale by ceil(losses/starting strength*50). A surviving damaged formation checks d100 against its current morale; above morale retreats. A destroyed formation has zero strength. Do not invent extra casualties or reverse recorded defeat.

The current snapshot retains the latest 50 battle reports; prior full reports remain in the corresponding save-history versions. Compact permanent receipt hashes prevent an archived phase or achievement from being paid/rolled again. Inspect historical versions only when an actual past fact is needed. The phase resolver executes the written base formation rules. Novel class-specific power effects remain GM-adjudicated with their actual costs and checks, committed before the affected phase; merely recording an ability does not automatically execute arbitrary prose as server code.

After each phase compare active strength on the front: only a side with greater active strength earns one objective progress. A tied phase earns none. Reaching published target_progress or eliminating/routing every opposing formation closes the front. Keep rolls, losses, objective progress and outcome in the battle report. Default significant fronts to 2..3 objective progress, and stop for a material new player decision rather than dragging out repetitive phases. Victory awards 40 formation XP to survivors, defeat 20, a fought unresolved phase 10; commanders earn twice the assigned formation award, once per commander per phase batch. Other progression awards must refer to a separate actual achievement.

Deaths, capture, losses, territory changes and recovery follow the actual class mechanics and shared fiction. Recovery needs its established cause/resources/time. A destroyed army, dead general or fallen domain can have lasting consequences. Do not presume summons disappear with the sovereign's death unless their owned mechanics say so. Keep personal power exceptional against lesser threats and let capable rivals make coherent strategic decisions.
