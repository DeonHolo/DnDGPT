# Shared scenery refresh

Runtime 1.1.6 replaces the 30 older mixed-resolution artwork options with 21 landscape paintings by Andreas Rocha. The retained choices are Glowwood, two plain themes and all 15 D&D scenic views. All 39 choices are available in every game mode, with D&D wallpapers first, Fantasy landscapes second and Reading atmospheres last. Per-game preferences stay independent.

New files are the artist's original published WebPs, 2,500–3,840 pixels wide and 1,137–1,920 pixels tall. They are neither resized nor recompressed; source URLs, native dimensions, hashes, byte counts and artist credits are recorded in ARTWORK_SOURCES.json. The scene selector displays the artist and links to the original work. Copyright is labeled honestly, without claiming an open license or endorsement. Selection was based on landscape composition and visual review, not a free-license-only filter. Large character figures, process sheets, text banners and the especially narrow panorama were excluded. Small environmental scale details and artist signatures are retained.

Glowwood is byte-identical to both its prior dashboard file and the website image. The website tree background is unchanged. Removed background preferences fall back to Classic in the UI; reading a campaign does not rewrite it. The existing separately served content-hashed asset route prevents thumbnail/base64 size-limit regressions. No image payload or lookup is added to gameplay calls.

Local verification: 15 scene tests pass, including attribution/hash integrity, anonymous immutable asset responses, per-game sharing, native dimensions and the preserved tree. The in-app browser passed all 14 D&D checks, including decoding every new image and every retained D&D image at its native resolution, shared selection in both homebrew modes, independent preferences and layout readability at narrow/wide widths. These use disposable data, not player campaigns.
